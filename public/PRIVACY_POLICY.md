# Privacy Policy for Walking Challenge

**Last Updated:** December 30, 2025

## Introduction

Walking Challenge ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Walking Challenge mobile application and web service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (via Google Sign-In)
- Display name
- Profile picture (optional)

### Health Data
- Step count from Apple Health (iOS) or Samsung Health Connect (Android)
- Walking distance
- Active energy burned
- Date and time of health data sync

### Usage Data
- Challenge participation
- Progress tracking
- Leaderboard rankings
- Encouragement messages sent/received

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Track your progress in walking challenges
- Display leaderboards and rankings
- Send encouragement messages to challenge participants
- Generate personalized insights about your activity
- Sync health data across your devices

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore
- Health data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
- We use Google OAuth for secure authentication
- We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties

## Health Data Permissions

### iOS (HealthKit)
We request permission to read:
- Step count
- Walking/running distance
- Active energy burned

### Android (Health Connect)
We request permission to read:
- Steps
- Distance
- Total calories burned

**You control these permissions** and can revoke them at any time through your device settings.

## Data Sharing

We **do not share** your health data or personal information with:
- Third-party advertisers
- Data brokers
- Marketing companies
- Other users (except anonymized leaderboard rankings)

The only data visible to other challenge participants:
- Your display name
- Your step count totals in shared challenges
- Your leaderboard ranking

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Export your health data
- Opt out of specific data collection
- Revoke health app permissions at any time

## Data Retention

- Account data: Retained until you delete your account
- Health sync data: Last 7 days automatically synced
- Challenge history: Retained for 1 year after challenge ends
- Deleted accounts: All data permanently removed within 30 days

## Children's Privacy

Walking Challenge is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending an email notification (for significant changes)
- Displaying an in-app notification

## Legal Compliance

Walking Challenge complies with:
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
- Apple HealthKit Privacy Requirements
- Google Health Connect Privacy Requirements

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Google Firebase:** Data storage and authentication
- **Netlify:** Web hosting
- **Google OAuth:** Secure sign-in
- **Apple HealthKit:** iOS health data access (user-authorized)
- **Google Health Connect:** Android health data access (user-authorized)

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their use of data.

## Data Processing

Your health data is processed on:
- Your device (local processing)
- Our secure servers (Netlify + Firebase)
- **Never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing**

## International Users

Our services are hosted in the United States. By using Walking Challenge, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States.

By using Walking Challenge, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our collection and use of information as described.
